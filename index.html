07 / Common questions

Reasonable questions, straight answers.

What kind of work do you take on?

Websites, simple custom tools and dashboards, computer and network setup, email and backups, security improvements, and fixing whatever's slowing your business down. If you're not sure it's a fit, just ask—the first call is free.

I already have a website or system. Can you work with it?

Usually, yes. We'll look at what you have, tell you honestly what's worth keeping, and suggest the simplest way forward—improve, extend, or start fresh.

Do I need to know the technical words?

No. Describe the problem the way you'd explain it to a neighbor. Sorting out the technical part is our job.

What does a job cost?

It depends on the work, but you'll always get a clear plan and price in writing before anything starts. No surprise invoices, and no charge for the first conversation.

Do you offer help after the job is done?

Yes. We can stay available for questions, upkeep, and improvements. Many customers prefer an ongoing hand rather than us vanishing after the install.

Can you help after a hack, virus, or lost files?

Yes—and please call before wiping or reinstalling anything, since that can destroy the very thing that helps us recover your data. Start with our what-to-do-first guide, then get in touch.

Do you do forensics or penetration testing?

Yes, as specialist engagements with a defined question, proper authorization, and a clear scope. It's the background the rest of our work stands on, and it stays available when you need it.

What should I include in my first message?

A short description of the problem or goal, and how to reach you. Please don't send passwords or sensitive files through the website form—we'll set up a safe way to share anything like that.
