Services

Most work falls into three areas: investigating an incident, preventing the next one, and keeping your data recoverable.

forensics

Digital forensics

Acquisition and analysis of computers, drives, and phones with a documented chain of custody. Useful for employee misconduct, data theft, and civil or family matters.

In plain English: We find out what is on a device and what happened to it, and document that we did not change anything.

response

Incident response

Containment, root-cause analysis, and recovery after ransomware, a compromised email account, or unauthorized access. You get a written timeline of what happened.

In plain English: Something went wrong. We stop the damage, find out how it happened, and help you get running again.

assessment

Security assessments

A practical review of your network, accounts, backups, and remote access, with findings ranked by real risk and a fix list you can act on.

In plain English: A checkup for your network and accounts, ranked so you fix the scary stuff first.

hardening

Hardening and secure remote access

VPNs, multi-factor authentication, firewall and network segmentation, and endpoint cleanup for small offices, shops, and clinics.

In plain English: Better locks on the doors you forgot you had, including a safe way to work from home.

backup

Backup and recovery planning

On-premise and offline backups you have actually tested, so a bad day is an inconvenience rather than the end of the business.

In plain English: Copies of your data that are proven to work before you need them.

recovery

Data recovery and sanitization

Recovery from failed or damaged drives where possible, and verified wiping of retired equipment so old data stays gone.

In plain English: Getting files off failing drives, and making sure retired ones are truly blank.

How an engagement works

Forensic work is only useful if it holds up. Every case follows the same discipline.

  1. Scope and consultConfidential conversation about what happened, what you need, and who will see the results.
  2. PreserveForensic copies are made so original evidence is never altered. Everything is logged and hashed.
  3. AnalyzeTimeline, artifacts, and account activity are examined against the questions you need answered.
  4. ReportA clear written report with findings, limits of what the evidence can show, and recommended next steps.
  5. RemediateOptional hands-on cleanup and hardening so the same hole is closed.
