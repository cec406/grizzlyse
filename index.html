02 / How we work

The same engineer stays with the project through rollout.

You'll work directly with Caleb Clark, the engineer behind Grizzly. Together we'll agree on what's changing, how we'll know it works, and who handles it after launch.

  1. 01 / Discover

    Walk through a real day of work

    Find the bottleneck, see what the team already relies on, and agree on what a good outcome looks like.

  2. 02 / Design & build

    Get the plan and price in writing

    Agree on scope, ownership, and how we'll tell if the system is ready. Then build or integrate and test the workflows that matter.

  3. 03 / Deploy

    Roll out with the people who use it

    Agree on a fallback before switching over. Verify a real task from the devices your team uses, not just from the machine hosting the software.

  4. 04 / Support

    Leave the team ready to operate it

    Walk operators through daily tasks and recovery. Agree on what your team handles and what Grizzly supports after handoff.
