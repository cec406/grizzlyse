03 / The engagement

No mystery.
No magic claims.

Good technical work should leave you with more clarity—not more questions about what you paid for.

What we aim to deliver

  • A scope agreed before work begins.
  • Documented methods and evidence handling.
  • Findings separated from uncertainty.
  • Practical recommendations tied to your situation.

Exact deliverables depend on the engagement.

  1. Talk & scope

    Discuss the situation, authorization, priorities, and who needs the results. Agree on the work before proceeding.

  2. Preserve & document

    Choose an appropriate collection method, document handling, and verify copies where applicable. Record collection limitations.

  3. Investigate

    Examine relevant artifacts, timelines, and account activity against the questions that matter.

  4. Explain

    Report findings in plain language, including what is supported, what is uncertain, and recommended next steps.

  5. Improve

    Where agreed, help with recovery, cleanup, and hardening to address the issues identified.

07 / Before you reach out

A few reasonable questions.

Do I need to know exactly what's wrong?

No. Start with what you noticed, when it began, and what you need help understanding. We'll discuss whether the issue fits our services.

Can you work with my attorney?

Yes, engagements can be discussed through counsel. Scope, authorization, reporting, and access to findings should be agreed before collection or analysis.

Can you guarantee a recovery or a particular finding?

No. Device condition, available records, encryption, and other limitations can affect what can be recovered or established. We aim to be clear about limitations rather than promise an outcome.

What should I send in my first message?

A brief description, a safe way to contact you, and whether the issue is time-sensitive. Please don't send passwords, evidence files, or sensitive case details through the general inquiry form. We can discuss an appropriate transfer method.

Does the terminal do anything to my device?

The mini-terminal only responds to predefined commands in this page. It does not execute operating-system commands or scan your device. Hashing uses your browser's Web Crypto API when available.
