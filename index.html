01 / Services

What we engineer and deploy

For shops, contractors, and operations teams that need a working system, not just a recommendation. We can build something new or improve what you already have.

Software & integration

Tools that fit the workflow

Replace scattered spreadsheets and hand-entered reports with job tracking, shop-floor dashboards, or automation that connects the tools you already use.

Infrastructure & rollout

Systems set up where you need them

Deploy applications on your existing network or a hosted environment. Plan configuration, access, backups, and cutover around how your team works.

Help after launch

02 / How we work

The same engineer stays with the project through rollout.

We work directly with the people who will use and maintain the system. You know what's changing, how we'll check that it works, and who owns it once it's live.

  1. 01 / Discover

    Walk through a real day of work

    Find the bottleneck, see what the team already relies on, and agree on what a good outcome looks like.

  2. 02 / Design & build

    Get the plan and price in writing

    Agree on scope, ownership, and how we'll tell if the system is ready. Then build or integrate and test the workflows that matter.

  3. 03 / Deploy

    Roll out with the people who use it

    Schedule the change, verify it in the target environment, and plan how to revert if it doesn't work as expected.

  4. 04 / Support

    Leave the team ready to operate it

    Walk operators through daily tasks and recovery. Agree on what your team handles and what Grizzly supports after handoff.

03 / Start a project
