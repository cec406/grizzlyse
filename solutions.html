04 / The engagement

No mystery. No magic claims.

  1. Talk & scope

    Discuss the situation, priorities and who needs the results. Agree on the work before proceeding.

  2. Preserve & document

    Choose an appropriate method, document handling, and verify copies where applicable.

  3. Investigate & improve

    Examine the relevant artifacts against the questions that matter, then act on what we find.

  4. Explain

    Report findings in plain language — what is supported, what is uncertain, and the next step.
