# STATION DEMOKRATIYA

*The last metro station under the ash. The Ministry says you are free. The bugs say otherwise.*

A typing-defense game where you learn Russian and Spanish by killing bugs. Each bug carries a word. Type what it means and it dies. Let it reach the gate and the Ministry will make you learn its name.

## Running the game

1. Save `station-demokratiya.html` anywhere on your computer.
2. Double-click it. It opens in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari).
3. Click **ENLIST**.

It needs a keyboard, so it won't work on a phone. It works offline. Without internet only the fonts change, and the game plays the same. Your best score is saved in that browser.

## Goal

Defend the gate for as many waves as you can. The gate starts at 100 integrity. Every bug that reaches it costs you 12. At 0 the gate falls and the run ends.

## Choosing what to practice

Pick a mode from the dropdown on the start screen:

| Mode | A bug shows | You type |
|---|---|---|
| Cyrillic letters → Latin sounds | Ж | `zh` |
| Russian → English | Жук | `bug` |
| Russian → Spanish | Вода | `agua` |
| Spanish → English | fuego | `fire` |
| English → Spanish | water | `agua` |

Accents don't matter. Type `n` for `ñ` and `u` for `ú`. Case doesn't matter either.

## Controls

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **A–Z** | Type your answer. It appears at the bottom of the screen. |
| **Enter** | Submit the answer right now. |
| **Backspace** | Delete the last letter. |
| **Esc** | Clear what you've typed — or, with nothing typed, pause. |
| **Arrow keys** | Enter stratagem codes (see below). |

While paused you can reopen the **FIELD GLOSSARY**: every word in the current practice mode with the answers the game accepts. Use it between waves to study.

## Killing bugs

- Bugs crawl toward the gate along five lanes. The word on the bug is your prompt.
- As you type, bugs whose answer starts with your letters light up in amber.
- The answer fires automatically once your letters match a bug's answer completely. If a longer answer is still possible (for example, `sh` when a bug also wants `shch`), it waits for you. Press **Enter** to fire anyway.
- If two bugs share an answer, the one closest to the gate dies first.
- If you type a letter that can't start any answer on screen, your typing clears, your combo resets, and the screen shakes.

## Scoring

- Each kill is worth 10 points, multiplied by your combo: **x1** at the start, **x2** after 5 kills in a row, **x3** after 10, and so on.
- A wrong key or a bug reaching the gate resets your combo.
- Clearing a wave gives a bonus of 50 points times the wave number.
- Referendum policies can raise or lower your score multiplier.

## Stratagems

You earn **1 stratagem charge for every 8 kills**, and you start with 1. Enter a code with the arrow keys. Each takes four presses, and the code resets if you pause for about a second.

| Code | Name | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **↑ → ↓ →** | Orbital Liberty Strike | Destroys every bug on screen. No points, and your combo doesn't grow. |
| **← ↓ ↑ →** | Gate Repair | Restores 30 gate integrity. |

Save the strike for when the lanes are full. Use the repair before the gate gets critical. The gate turns red below 35.

## The Referendum

After each wave, the Ministry lets "the people" choose one of three policies (picked at random from six). Whatever you choose passes with 97 to 99.9% approval. Turnout is mandatory.

| Policy | Effect |
|---|---|
| Liberty Tax | +2 stratagem charges, gate -20 |
| Mandatory Optimism | Bugs 12% slower, score x0.85 |
| Volunteer Draft | Gate +35, 15% more bugs |
| Ministry of Truth | Bugs show the first letter of the answer, score x0.9 |
| Super Lottery | Coin flip: gate +50 or gate -30 |
| Double Rations of Freedom | Score x1.5, bugs 15% faster |

Policies stack for the whole run. The gate never goes above 100, and the Lottery or Liberty Tax can't kill you outright.

## How the game teaches you

- **Breaches teach.** When a bug reaches the gate, its prompt and answer appear in the log at the bottom of the screen.
- **Missed words return.** Words that got through come back more often, about a third of all bugs, until you kill them.
- **The end screen shows the damage.** When the gate falls, you'll see the words that got past you. Read them before you re-enlist.

## Tips

- Don't panic-type. A wrong letter costs your combo, and a clean combo is where most points come from.
- Keep your eyes on the lane nearest the gate.
- Start in the Cyrillic mode if the alphabet is new to you. Then move to the vocabulary modes.
- If you like Russian but want a break from reading it, switch to Spanish → English.
- Pick Ministry of Truth if you're still learning the words. The first-letter hint helps a lot.
