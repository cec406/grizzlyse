Proven Capabilities

Hardware Reclamation

The Slabtop & OptiPlex Conversions

We don't throw away good silicon. By stripping damaged laptops (like the MSI Raider) and upgrading older office towers (like the OptiPlex), we create highly efficient, low-cost servers. This means we can deploy reliable network storage and automation to your business without forcing you to buy expensive new server racks.

Secure Networking

Encrypted Mesh (Tailscale)

Traditional VPNs are clunky and prone to failure. We build peer-to-peer encrypted mesh networks. This allows your remote field crews or home-office workers to access the main shop's databases and files securely from anywhere, bypassing standard firewall vulnerabilities.

Private Intelligence

On-Premise AI Deployment

Public AI tools feed your private business data to the cloud. We deploy containerized intelligence models that run entirely on your local hardware. This provides your team with powerful automation and coding assistance while ensuring your proprietary data never leaves your building.
